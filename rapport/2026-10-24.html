LECTURE RÉGIME

In a down-cycle characterized by scarce fresh money and global regulatory tightening (MiCA, Taiwan crypto law, US GENIUS Act), Robinhood Chain represents the "Compliance Bridge." The chain's structural value proposition is not technological novelty but regulatory safety—offering institutional-grade tokenization within a broker-dealer wrapper. However, the current regime reveals a critical vulnerability: regulatory legitimacy is necessary but not sufficient when corporate issuers (Microsoft, AMC) actively block tokenization. The chain is currently absorbing "sticky" capital (lending/risk curation) but struggles to attract "explosive" capital due to the issuer opt-out risk. Structurally, Robinhood Chain is the testbed for whether regulated RWA can scale against issuer resistance; if the objections persist, the chain risks becoming a walled garden for synthetic perps rather than a venue for true asset tokenization.
