LECTURE RÉGIME

In a down-cycle characterized by scarce fresh capital and global regulatory tightening (MiCA, Taiwan crypto law, US GENIUS Act), Robinhood Chain represents the institutionalization of DeFi. The structural flow is no longer retail-driven speculation but regulated entry via tokenized real-world assets (RWAs). The chain's reliance on a regulated broker-dealer status allows it to navigate the "compliance moat," attracting capital that is barred from unregulated venues. However, the 40% transaction slowdown highlights the risk of friction in compliant systems. The emergence of competitors like Securitize on Solana suggests that "regulated" is no longer a unique differentiator; execution speed and liquidity depth are now the critical battlegrounds for institutional capital allocation.
