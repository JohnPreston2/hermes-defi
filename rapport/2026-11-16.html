ROBINHOOD CHAIN — DAILY 2026-11-15

ÉTAT ON-CHAIN

Chain TVL stands at $1.01B, recording a daily decline of -1.4% and extending a downward trajectory from the $1.08B peak observed on Nov 10 (chain_tvl_history). The capital structure remains highly concentrated: Morpho Blue commands ~59% of total chain TVL ($599.6M), with a utilization rate of ~75% ($451M borrowed / $599M supplied). The top three protocols—Morpho Blue, Steakhouse Financial, and Uniswap V4—account for approximately 75% of all locked capital. This concentration indicates a "barbell" distribution where capital efficiency is clustered in regulated lending and risk curation, while long-tail DeFi (Aero Slipstream, Metric V1) remains marginal. The current drawdown suggests initial launch incentives are tapering, exposing the stickiness of core utility versus speculative liquidity.

CATALYSEURS

  • Institutional Tokenization Race: The selection of Avalanche by the NYSE for tokenized stock settlement and DTCC's commitment to Solana for tokenized treasuries (2027) establishes a competitive backdrop for Robinhood Chain. While these rival venues capture headlines, Robinhood's direct backing of its own stock tokenization on OKX-ICE provides immediate on-chain utility, likely anchoring the early TVL observed in Morpho and Uniswap pools.
  • Regulatory Friction (Louisiana Litigation): The lawsuit filed by Louisiana against Kalshi and Robinhood regarding prediction markets introduces headline risk. This development underscores the "regulated broker" paradox: while Robinhood Chain benefits from institutional legitimacy, it remains subject to fragmented jurisdictional attacks (US GENIUS Act, MiCA compliance). This friction likely contributes to the conservative capital allocation currently observed, with capital favoring established lending primitives over higher-risk prediction or derivative instruments.
  • Protocol Maturity: The dominance of Steakhouse Financial ($537M TVL) alongside Morpho signals that sophisticated risk management is a primary driver of chain activity, rather than pure speculative DEX volume.

VALUE ACCRUAL

As Robinhood Chain operates without a native token, value capture is entirely protocol-specific and dictated by on-chain metrics:

  • Spark (SPK): Captures 30.5% of protocol revenue (measured), demonstrating viable yield generation in the savings category despite lower TVL ($7.7M) compared to lending giants.
  • Lighter (LIT): Captures 62.1% of protocol revenue (measured), indicating strong fee-to-valuation conversion in the derivatives sector ($111M TVL), likely driven by the perps product integration.
  • Uniswap (UNI): Captures 100% of protocol revenue (measured), retaining full value from its DEX operations ($126M TVL on V4).
  • Morpho (MORPHO): Value accrual is currently captation non mesuree (revenu du protocole inconnu), meaning despite commanding ~59% of chain TVL, the specific revenue capture mechanism for the token is not quantified in the current dataset.

LECTURE RÉGIME

In a down-cycle characterized by scarce fresh money and global regulatory tightening (MiCA, Taiwan crypto law, US GENIUS Act), Robinhood Chain represents the "Compliance L2" thesis. The structural flow is not retail speculation but institutional capital seeking compliant exposure to tokenized equities and RWAs. The current TVL composition—dominated by Morpho lending and Steakhouse risk curation—reflects a regime where capital quality (stickiness, regulatory safety) outweighs yield chasing. The chain is positioning itself as the infrastructure for regulated tokenization, distinct from the speculative volatility of unpermissioned chains. The competitive pressure from Avalanche (NYSE) and Solana (DTCC) forces Robinhood Chain to rely on its broker-dealer status to secure liquidity, making regulatory navigation the primary moat.
