ROBINHOOD CHAIN — DAILY 2026-11-19

ÉTAT ON-CHAIN

Chain TVL stands at $990M (-0.1% 24h), stabilizing after a 6% drawdown from the Nov 12 peak of ~$1.08B. The trajectory indicates a consolidation phase following initial launch volatility. Capital quality is heavily skewed towards sticky, institutional-grade protocols: Morpho Blue commands ~60.3% of total chain TVL ($597M), with a high utilization rate of 75.4% ($450M borrowed), signaling robust demand for leverage. Steakhouse Financial, acting as the risk curator, holds ~$535M, reinforcing the concentration of capital in managed risk strategies rather than speculative liquidity farming. Derivatives penetration is maturing, with Lighter Robinhood Perps ($101M) and Arcus Perps ($57M) capturing ~16% of market share, while DEX liquidity remains fragmented between Uniswap V4 ($122M) and V3 ($63M).

CATALYSEURS

  • Regulatory Headwinds (Insider Trading & Compliance): The guilty plea of a former Robinhood engineer for insider trading on Hyperliquid (Decrypt) serves as a reputational shock, potentially tightening internal compliance gates ahead of full institutional rollout. This coincides with heightened scrutiny from US Senators regarding money laundering risks in tokenized stocks (CoinTelegraph), which may enforce stricter KYC/AML on-ramps, temporarily dampening retail inflows.
  • Structural Standardization: The Coinbase-Broadridge proposal for tokenized stock disclosures (CoinTelegraph) and DTCC's planned 2027 entry onto Solana (Decrypt) validate the asset class but commoditize the infrastructure. This pressures Robinhood Chain to accelerate differentiation via superior execution (dYdX Arcus) and native liquidity (Uniswap V4) rather than relying on novelty.
  • Legal Friction: The Louisiana lawsuit against Robinhood and Kalshi regarding prediction markets (The Block) highlights the jurisdictional friction between regulated brokerages and emerging on-chain markets, creating a cautious overhang on expansion into new synthetic asset classes.
  • Cross-Chain Liquidity: The reported liquidity injection into tokenized stocks on Monad via Uniswap V4 (The Defiant) suggests a competitive landscape where RH Chain must defend its 24/7 stock narrative against alternative L2s attracting similar liquidity.

VALUE ACCRUAL

Robinhood Chain operates without a native token, resulting in "captation non mesuree" at the chain level; value accrual is entirely externalized to the infrastructure protocols deployed upon it. Based on the measured token capture table:

  • Spark (SPK): Captures 65.4% of protocol revenue, dominating the yield-bearing segment ($8.1M TVL).
  • Lighter (LIT): Captures 63.7% of fees, securing the perps revenue stream ($101M TVL).
  • Uniswap (UNI): Captures 100% of swap fees, aggregating value from both V3 and V4 liquidity pools ($185M combined TVL).
  • Morpho (MORPHO): Remains "NON MESUREE" (revenu du protocole inconnu) despite holding the majority of chain TVL, obscuring the fee-yield mechanics of the dominant lending market.

LECTURE RÉGIME

In a down-cycle characterized by scarce fresh capital and global regulatory tightening (MiCA, US GENIUS Act, Taiwan crypto laws), Robinhood Chain represents the "compliance moat" thesis. The structural flow is not from speculative degens, but from regulated institutions seeking DeFi yields without counterparty risk. The dominance of Steakhouse Financial and Morpho indicates a preference for risk-managed, curated capital allocation over pure DEX speculation. While regulatory noise (Senators, Louisiana lawsuits) creates friction, it ultimately acts as a barrier to entry for unregulated competitors, reinforcing Robinhood's position as the compliant conduit for tokenized RWAs. The chain's value proposition shifts from "growth" to "safe harbor" for institutional liquidity.

  • Morpho Utilization: Sustained borrowing rates above 75%; a drop would signal de-leveraging pressure.
  • Lighter vs. Arcus Market Share: Shifts in perps volume between Lighter ($101M) and Arcus ($57M) as a proxy for trader preference for order-book vs. AMM execution.
  • Steakhouse Risk Parameters: Any adjustments to risk curation settings that could trigger capital reallocation within the Morpho vaults.
