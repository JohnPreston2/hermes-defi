LECTURE RÉGIME

In a down-cycle characterized by scarce fresh money and global regulatory tightening (MiCA, US GENIUS Act, Taiwan frameworks), Robinhood Chain represents the "compliance trade." The structural flow is not speculative retail degeneracy, but institutional and high-quality retail capital seeking on-chain exposure to RWAs without counterparty risk. The regulatory objections against competitors (OKXICE) validate the moat of Robinhood's regulated broker status. The chain serves as a walled garden where tokenized stocks and derivatives can operate within existing legal guardrails, attracting capital that is otherwise barred from unpermissioned DeFi. The high concentration in top protocols suggests capital is sticky and risk-averse, clustering around audited, blue-chip infrastructure (Morpho, Uniswap) rather than chasing yield farms.

Dans un régime de rareté de l'argent frais crypto, la tokenisation d'actifs réls réglementés (fonds trésorerie, obligations, infrastructures de paiement) représente le vecteur d'adoption le plus robuste, attirant un capital de qualité qui privilégie la sécurité juridique (MiCA, licences Singapour/HK) au rendement spéculatif.
