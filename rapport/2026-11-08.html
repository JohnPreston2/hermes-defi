ROBINHOOD CHAIN — DAILY 2026-11-07

ÉTAT ON-CHAIN

Chain TVL stands at $1.09B, marking a +3.8% daily uptick, reversing the minor drawdown observed over the prior week (low of $1.05B on Nov 4). The trajectory shows resilience despite broader market stagnation. Capital concentration remains elevated, with the top 3 protocols—Morpho Blue ($609M), Steakhouse Financial ($534M), and Uniswap V4 ($165M)—commanding ~65% of total liquidity. Morpho maintains dominance in the lending sector, accounting for 55.9% of chain TVL in supply, with a utilization rate of 77% ($469M borrowed out of $609M supplied). This high utilization suggests robust demand for leverage within the regulated environment, though the heavy skew towards Steakhouse (risk curators) implies a significant portion of TVL is sophisticated, yield-seeking capital rather than purely retail liquidity.

CATALYSEURS

  • Regulatory Friction on Tokenized Equities: Major issuers (Amazon, Tesla, SpaceX, Nvidia, Costco, Nike) have formally objected to their tokenized stocks being traded on OKX-ICE (CoinDesk). This creates a structural headwind for the 24/7 tokenized stock narrative on Robinhood Chain, as corporate governance and securities law compliance (US GENIUS Act) become the primary bottleneck rather than technical capability. This friction explains the modest TVL growth in the equity segment relative to DeFi primitives.
  • Infrastructure Maturation: Uniswap's extension of the VolatilePair hook to Robinhood Chain (The Defiant) signals deepening technical integration. This allows for more efficient capital routing and custom AMM logic, directly supporting the +3.8% TVL jump and the rising prominence of Uniswap V4 ($165M TVL) as a liquidity hub.
  • Institutional Validation: Ark Invest's continued accumulation of Robinhood shares (The Block) reinforces the thesis that regulated broker-dealers are the preferred on-ramps for institutional capital in the current regime. This external validation supports the "sticky" nature of capital on-chain, particularly within risk-curated vaults like Steakhouse.
  • Traditional Finance Convergence: The NYSE partnership with Broadridge for tokenized shareholder voting (CoinDesk) highlights the parallel development of compliant infrastructure. While not directly on-chain for RH yet, it validates the regulatory pathway Robinhood Chain is pursuing, distinguishing it from unpermissioned competitors facing issuer pushback.

VALUE ACCRUAL

Robinhood Chain possesses no native chain token; value accrual is strictly protocol-level. Based on the measured token capture table:

  • Uniswap (UNI): Captures 100.0% of protocol revenue. The integration of VolatilePair hooks and the chain's focus on compliant DEX activity positions UNI to capture the fee yield from the increasing swap volume.
  • Lighter (LIT): Captures 77.2% of protocol revenue. As the dominant perps DEX on the chain ($115M TVL), Lighter is a primary beneficiary of the leverage demand evidenced by Morpho's high utilization.
  • Spark (SPK): Captures 36.2% of protocol revenue. While Spark Savings TVL ($11.7M) is smaller relative to Morpho, its yield-bearing nature attracts stable capital.
  • Morpho (MORPHO): Value accrual is NON MESUREE (revenu du protocole inconnu). Despite Morpho holding ~56% of chain TVL and driving the core lending activity ($469M borrowed), the current valuation board does not provide a measured revenue capture metric for the MORPHO token.

LECTURE RÉGIME

In a down-cycle characterized by scarce fresh money and global regulatory tightening (MiCA, Taiwan crypto law, US GENIUS Act), Robinhood Chain represents the "Compliance Moat" thesis. The structural flow is not from retail degens chasing yield, but from institutional and sophisticated capital seeking regulated exposure to crypto assets and tokenized equities. The issuer objections to OKX-ICE underscore that unregulated tokenization is a dead end; Robinhood's status as a regulated broker provides the only viable structural pathway for RWA (Real World Asset) tokenization. The high concentration in Steakhouse and Morpho indicates that capital on-chain is "sticky" and risk-aware, prioritizing compliant yield over speculative airdrop farming. The chain is effectively a walled garden where regulatory adherence is the product, not the feature.
