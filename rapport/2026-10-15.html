The dominance of Steakhouse Financial and Morpho suggests capital is seeking "safe" yield and risk-managed exposure rather than high-leverage speculation. Robinhood Chain is effectively positioning itself as the compliant bridge for TradFi capital, where the "regulatory moat" is the primary asset, even if it limits the explosive growth seen in permissionless chains.
