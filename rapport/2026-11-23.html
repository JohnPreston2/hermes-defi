ROBINHOOD CHAIN — DAILY 2026-11-22

ÉTAT ON-CHAIN

Chain TVL stands at $951.7M, recording a marginal daily increase of +0.5% after a multi-week drawdown from the $1.06B peak seen in early November. The trajectory indicates a stabilization phase following the recent capital flight. Morpho Blue remains the dominant capital sink, commanding $593M in supply TVL (62.3% of chain total) and $451M in borrowed assets, representing ~83% of total lending utilization. This concentration underscores a structural reliance on Morpho for leverage within the ecosystem. Protocols outside the top tier (Steakhouse, Uniswap V4, Lighter) show negligible TVL contribution, suggesting capital stickiness is currently concentrated in risk-curation and lending rather than fragmented DEX liquidity.

CATALYSEURS

  • Regulatory Clarity & Institutional Entry: Trump’s SEC nominee Johnson's backing for formal rulemaking on tokenized stocks (CoinTelegraph) provides a structural tailwind for Robinhood Chain's core value proposition. This regulatory signal directly supports the resilience observed in tokenized stock volumes despite broader market selloffs (The Block).
  • Tokenized Equity Velocity: High DEX volume in Take-Two tokenized shares, exceeding Nvidia (CoinTelegraph), coupled with anticipation for Anthropic’s IPO (CoinDesk), demonstrates that real-world asset (RWA) volume is driving on-chain activity, not speculative crypto-native assets.
  • Competitive Landscape & Compliance: Fairmint’s planned tokenized venue on Canton/Ethereum under SEC experiment (CoinDesk) validates the market but introduces competition. Concurrently, Democratic senators' concerns over money laundering risks (CoinTelegraph) highlight the regulatory friction points that Robinhood’s regulated status is designed to navigate.
  • Operational Security: The guilty plea of a former Robinhood engineer for crypto insider trading (Decrypt) serves as a critical reminder of internal control requirements, even as the chain maintains its bullish outlook against Kalshi (The Block).

VALUE ACCRUAL

Robinhood Chain operates without a native token, meaning protocol-level value accrual is entirely externalized to application layers. Based on the measured token capture table:

  • Uniswap (UNI): Captures 100% of protocol revenue, positioning it as the primary beneficiary of spot trading fees, particularly from tokenized stock swaps.
  • Lighter (LIT): Captures 74% of protocol revenue, deriving value from the perpetual futures market ($97M TVL).
  • Morpho (MORPHO): Value accrual is currently "NON MESUREE" (protocol revenue unknown), despite its dominance in TVL and borrowing; fee capture remains opaque in current data.
  • Spark (SPK): Data is incoherent; no conclusion can be drawn regarding value capture.

LECTURE RÉGIME

In a down-cycle characterized by scarce fresh money and tightening global regulation (MiCA, Taiwan crypto law, US GENIUS Act), Robinhood Chain represents the structural convergence of compliance and DeFi. The chain is absorbing capital not by offering higher yields than the broader market, but by providing a regulated venue for tokenized equities—a "safe harbor" for institutional and retail capital seeking exposure to RWAs without the counterparty risks of unregulated offshore venues. The dominance of Morpho and Uniswap suggests that in this regime, capital prefers established, compliant primitives over experimental, high-risk yield farms. The chain is effectively a compliance-wrapped liquidity layer for the new tokenized securities regime.

À SURVEILLER

  • Tokenized Stock Volumes: Specifically monitor Take-Two and Nvidia volume shifts on Uniswap V4 vs. CEXs as a proxy for RWA adoption.
  • SEC Rulemaking Progress: Any concrete steps from the Johnson-led SEC regarding tokenized stock trading frameworks.
  • Morpho Utilization Rates: Watch for a decline in the $451M borrowed figure, which would signal deleveraging pressure in the current risk-off environment.

RWA — DAILY 2026-11-22

ÉTAT DU SECTEUR

La qualité du capital reste majoritairement institutionnelle et "sticky" (modèles émetteurs type BlackRock/Circle), contrastant avec les segments plus volatils ou dépendants d'incitations de lancement.

CATALYSEURS

L'actualité réglementaire et institutionnelle des dernières 24 heures structure les flux observés :

  • Régulation (ECB/SEC) : Les avertissements de la présidente de la BCE, Christine Lagarde, sur la perte de contrôle souverain face à la tokenisation, couplés aux déclarations de Kristin Johnson (nommée à la SEC) favorisant une règle formelle pour la tokenisation, créent un climat d'attente. Cela explique la prudence (baisse de TVL) alors que les acteurs attendent des cadres juridiques clairs plutôt que de déployer du capital frais.
  • Produit (Ondo / Ant International) : Le lancement par Ondo d'ANTon, une représentation tokenisée 24/7 des actions Anthropic pour les investisseurs non-US, alimente la sous-narrative des "equities tokenisées". Parallèlement, le protocole "Whale Orchestration" d'Ant International pour le règlement temps réel entre dépôts tokenisés et fonds du marché monétaire signale une optimisation infrastructurelle qui soutient la trajectoire à long terme.
  • Institutionnel (Singapour) : Le règlement par Singapour d'une vente de billets tokenisés de 77,5 M$ via sa monnaie numérique de banque centrale (MNBC) valide l'adoption par les banques centrales et fournit un signal positif pour l'intégration des RWA dans la finance traditionnelle.

LECTURE RÉGIME

Le ressort structurel du secteur RWA demeure la tension entre l'innovation et la régulation. Les déclarations de Lagarde et de Johnson illustrent le passage d'une phase "Far West" à une phase de normalisation réglementaire (MiCA en Europe, Genius/Clarity Acts aux US). Dans un régime de "argent frais rare" (scarcity of fresh crypto money), les flux institutionnels vers la tokenisation ne sont pas une expansion de capital spéculatif, mais un transfert d'efficience. La clarté réglementaire est la condition *sine qua non* pour que ces capitaux institutionnels, actuellement à la marge, migrent massivement sur-chain. L'activité de Singapour (MNBC) confirme que les juridictions proactives captent l'avantage premier de cette infrastructure.
